# Personal Portfolio for the IoT/MCU class at HSBI Gütersloh Campus
Author: [Rachel Nokutenda Machiya](https://github.com/Nokhue02/database-portfolio)

Introduction

Welcome to my personal learning portfolio. This repository documents my academic journey, practical assignments, and learning experiences throughout the course. It serves as a record of my progress as I develop my understanding of databases, SQL, programming, and related technologies.

The main purpose of this portfolio is to demonstrate not only the tasks I have completed but also the knowledge and practical skills I have gained along the way. Each module focuses on different concepts and provides an opportunity to apply theoretical knowledge to practical situations.

Throughout the course, I have worked on activities involving database management, SQL queries, Entity-Relationship (ER) diagrams, data integrity, and the use of development tools such as GitHub, Visual Studio Code, Python, and SQLite. These activities have helped me understand how databases are designed, how information is stored and retrieved, and how problems can be identified and corrected.

This repository is organised into different modules, each containing relevant code, exercises, documentation, and supporting evidence. I have also included personal reflections to explain what I learned, the challenges I encountered, how I approached those challenges, and what I would like to improve in future modules.

Collaboration has also been an important part of my learning experience. Working with my pod members has allowed me to exchange ideas, discuss different approaches, and develop a better understanding of concepts that I initially found difficult. I have also used tools such as ChatGPT to support my learning, particularly when interpreting instructions, troubleshooting errors, and understanding unfamiliar technical concepts. However, one of my main goals is to become more confident in solving problems independently rather than relying entirely on AI-generated solutions.

As I progress through the course, I aim to improve my technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and confidence in working with databases and programming tools.

Overall, this portfolio represents both my completed work and my development as a learner. It highlights my achievements, challenges, and the lessons I have gained through practical experience.

Portfolio Objectives

The main objectives of this repository are:

To document my learning progress throughout the course.

To demonstrate my understanding of database concepts and SQL.

To present practical assignments, code, and supporting evidence.

To reflect on challenges, mistakes, and improvements.

To demonstrate collaboration and independent problem-solving.

To develop confidence in using technical tools and evaluating AI-generated solutions.
