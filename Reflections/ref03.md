# Module 3 Reflection

### Take-away

Module 3 was mainly about learning how to understand SQL queries instead of simply relying on an LLM to generate them. Before tackling the main tasks, we were introduced to different workstreams that helped us understand the concepts behind SQL queries. This made the tasks easier to approach as I progressed through the module. One of my biggest takeaways was that understanding how a query works is just as important as getting the correct results.

### What was good?

Because of the practice and knowledge gained from the previous modules, I found this module less challenging than the others. What I learned from the workstreams helped me understand several SQL concepts, such as SEARCH, SCAN, and EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN.

Another aspect that made the module easier to understand was the scenario presented at the beginning. The story gave the tasks a practical context and helped me understand why checking SQL queries is important rather than simply trusting the results generated by an LLM.

### What was difficult, and where did I struggle?

While working with my pod members, we initially struggled to understand the instructions for the first task. However, after seeking clarification from the professor, we gained a better understanding of what was expected.

Task 3 was also challenging because, at some point, even with the help of ChatGPT, I felt like I was going in circles. However, as I continued working through the task, I began to understand the importance of testing SQL queries rather than assuming they were correct. The experience also showed me that using an LLM does not automatically make a task easier if I do not fully understand what I am trying to achieve.

### Interaction with Peers and Instructor

Interacting with my peers and instructor was particularly helpful throughout this module. Although the tasks seemed to become more manageable as we progressed, there were still several concepts and instructions that were confusing.

The discussions within my pod helped because each member had a different understanding of certain aspects of the tasks. By sharing our ideas and asking questions, we were able to clarify concepts that might have been difficult to understand individually.

The professor's explanations also helped us overcome some of the challenges we encountered, especially when interpreting the task instructions.

### Conclusion

Overall, Module 3 helped me develop a better understanding of SQL, particularly how queries operate and how to interpret their execution plans. I also learned the importance of checking whether a query is correct, efficient, and safe before using it.

Most importantly, this module taught me that I should not immediately turn to an LLM whenever I encounter a difficult task. Instead, I should first try to understand the problem, use the knowledge I have gained, and then use an LLM as a supporting tool.

Moving forward, I want to become more confident in analysing SQL queries independently and comparing different approaches rather than simply accepting the first solution provided by an LLM.
