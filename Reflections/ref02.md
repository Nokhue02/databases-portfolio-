# Module 2 Reflection

### Take-away

In Module 2, I learned how to understand databases better, identify problems, and make improvements to database designs. At the beginning of the module, I was introduced to important concepts such as entities, attributes, and cardinalities. Understanding these terms helped me learn how to construct Entity-Relationship (ER) diagrams and identify the different relationships between entities.

One of my biggest takeaways was understanding how important database design is and how identifying potential problems early can help create better and more reliable databases.

### What was good?

Task 1 was relatively easy to understand because my pod had already discussed the different terms and concepts involved. This made it easier to understand what was required of us.

Another thing that helped was having discussions with a different pod, which gave us a broader understanding of the task and different perspectives on how to approach it.

To improve our understanding of ER diagrams, my pod and I also sought assistance from a friend who had some knowledge of the subject. This helped us understand the diagrams better and gave us more confidence when completing the tasks.

### What was difficult, and where did I struggle?

One of my main challenges was trying to limit my use of AI. Although this might seem unusual, I realised that many of the problems I encountered when running code or following instructions were caused by very small mistakes that I struggled to identify on my own.

As I continued using ChatGPT, I gradually became better at understanding instructions, identifying errors, and correcting them.

For example, in Task 2, we were required to identify defects in the provided code. Initially, I did not fully understand what I was supposed to do. However, with the help of ChatGPT and one of my colleagues, I was able to understand the task and work through it.

Another challenge was working with the terminal. There were times when my code would not run because I was using PowerShell instead of the SQLite terminal. This taught me the importance of understanding the environment in which I am working, rather than immediately assuming that there is something wrong with my code.

### Interaction with Peers and Instructor

Working with my pod was helpful throughout the module. However, there were times when we all had similar interpretations of the instructions, which made it difficult to identify where we were going wrong.

To overcome this, we involved someone from outside our pod who had a different perspective. This helped us understand the tasks more clearly and approach the remaining activities with greater confidence.

This experience showed me the importance of collaboration and being open to different ideas, especially when working on technical problems.

### Conclusion

Overall, Module 2 helped me develop a better understanding of databases, particularly database design, ER diagrams, and identifying defects in code.

I also learned how to approach technical tasks more carefully and how to troubleshoot problems instead of immediately assuming that the code itself is incorrect.

One important lesson I learned was to pay closer attention to spelling, commands, and instructions when working in the terminal. There were several occasions when I realised that my code was correct, but a missing word, typing error, or incorrect command prevented it from running.

Another important lesson was finding a balance between using AI for assistance and developing my own understanding. Although ChatGPT helped me overcome several challenges, I realised that it is important to understand the solutions rather than simply copying them.

I believe that the knowledge and experience gained from this module will help me approach future modules with greater confidence and independence.
