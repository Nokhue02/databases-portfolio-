# Module 1 Reflection

### What worked?

I was not present for the first session of Module 1, but with Trevor's assistance, I was able to create my repository using the course template and clone it into VS Code. Fortunately, I already had most of the required software installed, which made the setup process easier.

I joined a Task Pod with Tinashe, Tashinga, and Trevor. Together, we discussed databases, SQL, and the local-first approach. My main challenge was understanding the module instructions and what was expected of me. Since I had missed the first session, I initially struggled to follow some of the activities. To overcome this, I used ChatGPT for guidance and relied on explanations from my pod members.

However, as I continued practising and discussing the game with my pod members, I gradually began to understand the concepts and what was expected.

This experience taught me that sometimes understanding a task takes patience and practice rather than immediately finding the correct answer.

### How did my understanding change?

This module helped me realise that learning programming is not only about writing code but also about understanding the tools used to develop and manage projects.

I also gained a basic understanding of local-first applications. Previously, I mainly thought of databases as centralised systems, but I now understand that applications can also store and manage data locally.

Although I have gained some knowledge of these concepts, I recognise that I still need to explore them further before I can confidently explain them independently.

### Collaboration and Support

Working with my pod was one of the most helpful parts of this module. Listening to everyone's different perspectives helped me understand the instructions better, especially during the SQL game.

The discussions also helped me realise that finding a solution is not always the most important part of learning. Understanding why something failed and how to correct it is equally important.

Having people to discuss problems with made the tasks less confusing and encouraged me to continue learning even when I struggled.

### Main Takeaway and Next Steps

My biggest takeaway from Module 1 was understanding the importance of active participation, collaboration, and learning from mistakes.

I realised that if I want to succeed in this course, I need to be actively involved in my pod, ask questions when I do not understand something, and be willing to make mistakes.

Rather than remaining passive or depending entirely on others for answers, I want to take more responsibility for my learning.

Moving forward, I have made a commitment to participate more actively, practise independently, and use the challenges I encounter as opportunities to improve my understanding and confidence.
